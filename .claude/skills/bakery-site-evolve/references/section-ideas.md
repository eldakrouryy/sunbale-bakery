# Bakery section ideas

Common sections on good bakery sites, and what facts each one needs. "Ready" means it can be
built today from `bakery-facts.md` alone; anything else needs the owner to provide info first.
Re-check against the current `bakery-facts.md` — the owner may have added facts since this was written.
